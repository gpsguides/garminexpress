Common Garmin GPS Issues

GPS signal problems

Move to an open outdoor area and allow the device time to acquire satellites. Obstructions and environmental conditions can affect signal reception.

Sync problems

Check Bluetooth, restart the app and device, and ensure the application is current.

Update problems

Confirm storage, internet connectivity and USB connection. Do not disconnect a device while an update is actively being installed.
