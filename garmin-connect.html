Garmin Connect

Explore general guidance for pairing compatible devices, syncing information and managing Garmin account features.

Pairing basics

Enable Bluetooth on the phone, open the compatible Garmin Connect application and follow the pairing prompts for your device.

Syncing

Keep the phone and device nearby, maintain a stable Bluetooth connection and allow synchronization to complete. Restarting both devices can help with temporary connection problems.
