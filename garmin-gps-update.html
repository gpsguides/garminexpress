GPS UPDATES

Garmin GPS Map Update Guide

Learn the general process for preparing a compatible Garmin GPS for map and software updates.

Before updating

Confirm your exact model, charge the device and use a reliable internet connection. Make sure there is enough storage available for the update.

Using Garmin Express

Garmin Express is a desktop application designed to manage supported Garmin devices. After installing the appropriate version, connect the GPS with a compatible USB cable and follow the application's instructions.

After the update

Allow the process to finish completely before disconnecting the device. Restart the GPS and verify that maps and software information appear correctly.

Important: Update availability and procedures vary by model and region. Verify details with official Garmin documentation.
