GETTING STARTED

Getting Started with Garmin GPS

Use this page as a starting point for understanding your device, navigation settings and software tools.

Initial setup

Charge the device, power it on and follow the on-screen setup instructions. Depending on the model, you may configure language, region, time, units and navigation preferences.

Connect your device

Compatible Garmin devices may connect through USB, Bluetooth or Wi-Fi. Keep the device charged and use the connection method recommended for your model.

Keep software current

Regular software and map updates can improve compatibility, routing information and device functionality. Check the documentation for your exact model before starting an update.

Tip: Always keep a backup of important data before major software changes.
